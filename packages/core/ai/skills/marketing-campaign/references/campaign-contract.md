# Campaign pack contract

Use only fields the request needs. A one-post request needs its finished post and a short rationale, while a launch across channels needs a reusable pack.

## Decision record

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Goal and conversion | One primary business action and its measure |
| Audience and situation | Buyer/user, need or occasion, geography if relevant |
| Platform × industry × method | One primary combination; separate rows for additional destinations or methods |
| Truth pack | Verified and user-provided facts with sources; assumptions and claim gaps clearly marked |
| Promise and proof | One audience-relevant promise supported by available evidence |
| Offer and CTA | Exact offer terms if known; one next action and destination |
| Channel execution | Native format, hook, asset/copy, landing or handoff, and required rights/approval |
| Measurement | Primary event, source of data, attribution caveat, decision point |

## Production handoff

For each requested asset, specify owner/platform, final copy or script, visual/audio needs, source evidence, format, CTA, status, and actual Knowledge reference when generated. Adapt structure and language to each destination instead of pasting one caption everywhere. Where a test is requested, change one primary variable at a time and define the decision rule before launch. Avoid invented forecasts or a fixed posting cadence without capacity and baseline evidence.

| Requested output | Minimum finished content |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign plan | Selected combination and rationale, audience, offer, channel sequence, asset list, owner/time assumptions, CTA, measurement and first decision point |
| Platform posts | Final text per destination, distinct hook/structure, source-backed claims, visual/cover brief, CTA and response handling |
| Paid ad draft | Ad copy/creative variants, audience and objective hypothesis, landing alignment, budget/schedule placeholders, conversion event and stop rule; clearly mark as not live |
| Landing page | Hero promise, proof, objection answers, CTA/form path and claim-to-source check |
| Email/SMS sequence | Segment and consent assumption, trigger, timing, full message copy, suppression, CTA, outcome and opt-out check |
| Search/local work | Query/intent map or listing changes, exact page/profile target, evidence, operational accuracy and measurement |
| Video campaign | Campaign purpose, platform, exact script, source-backed product/venue constraints, CTA and duration brief handed to `video-production`; attach its real canvas and rendered result only when returned |

For product promotion, keep a feature/claim ledger linked to current screenshots, docs, or product evidence. For restaurant promotion, keep menu, location, hours, offer and booking/order paths linked to current sources. A creative concept can be generated before all facts are known, but public-facing copy must expose unresolved placeholders instead of presenting them as truth.

## Decision and review

The primary metric should be the requested business action when observable. Add diagnostic metrics only to explain why the asset did or did not reach it. State the data source and attribution gap. Where a test is requested, define the single main change, target segment, guardrail, and continue/revise/stop rule. Avoid a promised outcome or arbitrary content volume.

The pack must distinguish `draft`, `ready for review`, `approved`, `published`, and `measured`. Approval of one asset does not approve another channel, external message, or spend. A scheduled, pending, or provider job is not a delivered asset.
