# Creators, partners, referrals, and loyalty

Use when trust or distribution is meaningfully improved by a third party or existing customer. Choose the mechanism deliberately:

| Mechanism | Fit | Required agreement or rule | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creator collaboration | Demonstration in an authentic audience context | Fit, exact product/venue facts, rights, deliverables, disclosure, compensation | Qualified traffic, leads/orders, content quality |
| Affiliate or channel partner | Partner can refer relevant buyers | Eligibility, attribution, payout terms, claim boundaries | Qualified referrals and fulfilled sales |
| Co-marketing | Complementary business shares an audience | Mutual value, ownership, approval, data handling | Registrations, inquiries, reciprocal value |
| Customer referral/loyalty | Satisfied customers have a reason to return/invite | Genuine value, eligibility, redemption and fraud/abuse controls | Repeat purchase and verified referrals |

Prepare a partner/creator brief containing audience fit, product/venue truth pack, non-negotiable claims, asset usage rights, disclosure needs, approval owner, CTA/tracking path, and reporting date. Let creators retain their own voice without inventing an endorsement or dictating a false personal experience. A sample outreach message is a draft; sending it is a separate authorized action.

Do not assume referral codes, rewards, partner accounts, customer consent, or private contact lists exist. Measure completed actions and partner quality rather than counting only posts or clicks.
