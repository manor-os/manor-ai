# Lifecycle email, SMS, and CRM

Use for a relationship with a legitimate permission and a meaningful trigger: welcome, activation, abandoned consideration where permitted, post-purchase care, rebooking, replenishment, win-back, or customer education. Select email/SMS/app/site as the destination separately. Never infer consent or an available list from the fact that a user has customers.

| Flow | Trigger and segment | Message job | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome/activation | New opted-in subscriber or user | Deliver promised value and first useful step | Activation or first purchase |
| Consideration follow-up | Qualified inquiry or demo interest | Answer one objection with evidence | Reply, demo, booking |
| Post-purchase/visit | Completed order or visit | Useful care, feedback or next occasion | Satisfaction and repeat action |
| Win-back | Eligible inactive customer | Relevant reason to return, with frequency control | Reactivated order/booking |

For each message supply segment, event trigger, delay, suppression/exclusion, subject/opening, full body when requested, CTA, destination, fallback, and success measure. Verify that offer dates, prices, and fulfillment remain true when the message sends. Review unsubscribe/opt-out and jurisdiction-specific messaging requirements through current account/policy sources before execution.

Measure the target action plus unsubscribes, complaints, replies, and deliverability where available. Opens are diagnostic, not proof of revenue. Do not schedule or send without the exact audience, payload, timing, channel, and authorization.
