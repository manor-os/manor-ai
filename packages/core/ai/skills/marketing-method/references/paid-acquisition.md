# Paid acquisition

Use only when the user requests advertising or the campaign recommendation has a justified budget path. A plan or draft is possible without spend authorization. An actual campaign requires the exact account, objective, audience/geo, budget cap, schedule, creative, destination, tracking, and approval through Manor's connected tools.

## Design the test

1. Define a conversion event near the business goal: qualified lead, booking, completed order, purchase, activated signup. Identify its tracking source and whether attribution is reliable.
2. Choose the platform and campaign type from the audience's intent and the connected account's current options. For search, align query → ad → page; for social/video, align audience/problem → creative → page. [Google Ads' official guide](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2567043?hl=en) describes campaign types by objective; verify options in the actual account.
3. Set the offer and landing path first. An ad cannot rescue an unavailable product, broken booking link, or unsupported claim.
4. Prepare a small test matrix: control asset, one materially different hook/proof/offer variant, audience/placement, budget and stop rule. Change one main variable at a time when interpreting results.
5. State what would count as evidence to continue, revise, or stop. If there is no baseline or expected conversion volume, report an exploratory test rather than inventing a forecast or promised CPA/ROAS.

## Handoff

Return campaign objective, exact destination, audience/geo, creative copy and asset references, landing URL, event/UTM plan, budget cap and schedule placeholders if unknown, approval state, and measurement plan. Distinguish `draft`, `ready for account review`, and `live`. Never claim spend or results without a provider receipt and observed metrics. Retargeting requires a usable consented audience and current platform eligibility.
