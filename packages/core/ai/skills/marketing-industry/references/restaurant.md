# Restaurant and food-service marketing

## First classify the transaction

Choose the actual business action: **dine-in/reservation**, **takeaway/delivery**, **catering/private event**, **menu or store opening**, or **repeat visit**. These need different offers and CTA paths. A “more awareness” brief still needs a reachable visit, booking, order, or inquiry route. For restaurant equipment sold to operators, also read `physical-product.md` and use a B2B lead route.

| Use case | Buyer moment | Content proof | CTA and measurement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dine-in | Choosing a time, group, cuisine or occasion | Real hero dishes, dining room, service, location, price range only if supplied | Reserve or get directions; booked covers or visits |
| Delivery/takeaway | Choosing a meal that arrives well | Actual portions, packaging, pickup/delivery area and timing if verified | Order via valid path; completed orders |
| Catering/private event | Planning for a group and budget | Menu packages, capacity, event setup, lead time and fulfillment details | Qualified inquiry; signed booking |
| Opening/menu launch | Checking what is new and when | Exact launch date, menu availability, real venue or approved preview | Visit/reserve/order; launch-period conversion |
| Retention | Repeating a liked experience | Genuine customer segment/occasion, new item or loyalty benefit | Return booking/order; repeat rate |

## Truth pack and creative

Inventory brand spelling, address, hours, reservation and order links, menu with prices/availability, dish names, allergens/dietary claims, current photos, licensed music/images, date-bound offer terms, and any genuine review permissions. Separate confirmed items from `[menu check]`, `[price check]`, and `[offer dates]` placeholders. Do not use generated food visuals as evidence for a real menu item unless the depiction is approved and faithful.

Creative should let a viewer identify the food or experience, who it is for, where/when it is available, and what to do next. For a video brief, specify the real dish/venue shots, plating and portion continuity, service action, ambience, natural sound, on-screen offer, and booking/order ending. The Video Production skill owns the actual canvas, generation, editing, and render. For a static/social brief, supply native caption and visual directions with the same factual identity.

## Quality and measurement

Check that menu/offer details match the landing page and that the restaurant can fulfill the promised time, location, capacity, and item. Measure the closest real action available: booking, completed order, call, catering lead, or repeat visit. Views and saves can diagnose creative but do not equal diners. Keep venue data and customer testimonials attributable to supplied evidence.
