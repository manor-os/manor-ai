# Solo Business Review Scorecard

Use the scorecard after the hard-blocker screen. It makes tradeoffs visible; it
does not convert assumptions into facts.

## Calculation

Score every dimension from 0 to 5, then calculate:

`weighted points = score / 5 × weight`

Round only the final total to a whole number. Show each raw score, weight,
evidence level, and rationale.

## Dimensions

### 1. Problem proof — 20 points

- 0: problem is absent or contradicted
- 1: plausible assumption only
- 2: isolated statements or weak proxy
- 3: repeated problem plus current workaround
- 4: material recurring cost, consequence, or active solution search
- 5: repeated costly behavior or commitment tied to solving the problem

### 2. Customer specificity and reach — 12 points

- 0: no identifiable buyer or lawful route
- 1: broad segment and cold reach only
- 2: identifiable segment, channel unproven
- 3: at least ten representative prospects are reachable
- 4: repeatable relevant channel produces conversations
- 5: owned/warm access already reaches decision-makers at useful frequency

### 3. Urgency and current alternatives — 10 points

- 0: no trigger and doing nothing has little consequence
- 1: nice-to-have aspiration
- 2: occasional inconvenience or weak substitute knowledge
- 3: recurring trigger and visible workaround
- 4: deadline, cost, risk, or revenue consequence motivates action
- 5: buyer is actively replacing or paying to manage the current alternative

### 4. Founder advantage and trust — 12 points

- 0: no capability, access, credibility, or viable learning path
- 1: generic interest
- 2: adjacent experience
- 3: relevant delivery proof or trusted access
- 4: hard-won insight plus credible outcomes and relationships
- 5: uncommon proof, owned assets, or privileged lawful access that compounds

### 5. Offer, willingness to pay, and economics — 14 points

- 0: no payer or economics cannot work
- 1: vague offer and invented price
- 2: comparable price or stated interest only
- 3: buyer incurs relevant cost and offer has plausible positive margin
- 4: price conversation, deposit, LOI, paid adjacent work, or paid pilot
- 5: repeated payment/renewal with founder labor and direct costs included

### 6. Solo operability — 14 points

- 0: requires unavailable team, license, capital, inventory, or constant support
- 1: workload grows almost one-for-one with customers
- 2: heavy customization, synchronous delivery, or exception load
- 3: bounded service with a credible capacity limit
- 4: standardized delivery, quality controls, and automation reduce marginal work
- 5: workload, support, and failure recovery remain sustainable at the founder's goal

### 7. Repeatability and leverage — 8 points

- 0: every delivery starts from zero
- 1: bespoke work dominates
- 2: some reusable assets
- 3: repeatable process and productization path
- 4: owned content, code, data, distribution, or automation drives most value
- 5: each customer or cycle improves a lawful compounding asset

### 8. Dependency and downside resilience — 10 points

- 0: known fatal dependency or unacceptable downside
- 1: one platform, supplier, customer, model, or policy can end the business
- 2: major dependency without a credible fallback
- 3: dependency is bounded or partially mitigated
- 4: portable assets, multiple routes, reversible spend, and clear stop-loss
- 5: low fixed cost, owned relationships, tested fallbacks, and limited blast radius

## Evidence caps

Apply caps before totaling:

- Problem evidence below E2: Problem proof maximum 2/5
- No observed current alternative or consequence: Urgency maximum 2/5
- No named path to ten representative prospects: Reach maximum 2/5
- Only hypothetical willingness-to-pay statements: Offer/economics maximum 2/5
- Founder hours and direct costs at ten customers unknown: Solo operability maximum 2/5
- Required IP/data/license rights unknown: Leverage maximum 2/5 and blocker flag
- Single external platform with no fallback: Resilience maximum 2/5
- No representative behavior or commitment evidence anywhere: overall verdict
  cannot exceed `REVISE BEFORE TEST`, regardless of total

## Hard blockers

Any confirmed fatal blocker overrides the numeric result:

- prohibited, unsafe, deceptive, or unlicensed core activity
- required credential or trust condition cannot be met
- buyer cannot be reached under the user's constraints
- negative unit economics even under a reasonable best case
- unavoidable capital or cash-cycle exposure exceeds loss limit
- delivery load exceeds capacity before the user's stated income goal
- critical data, IP, vendor, or platform dependency has no lawful fallback

Use `Specialist review required` instead of declaring a legal or regulated matter safe.

## Verdict thresholds

- **75–100: PROCEED TO TEST** — only with no hard blocker and at least E2 problem evidence
- **55–74: REVISE BEFORE TEST** — promising but a named design/evidence gap must be fixed
- **35–54: REFRAME** — preserve the strongest asset, buyer, or pain signal and narrow the idea
- **0–34: PARK / STOP** — current fit or evidence does not justify more work

Thresholds guide consistency. A clear hard blocker or unusually strong
contradicting evidence can lower the verdict. Explain any departure.
