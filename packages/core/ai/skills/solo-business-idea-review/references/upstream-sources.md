## Deliberate Manor adaptations

- The strongest positive verdict is `PROCEED TO TEST`, never `build now`.
- Scores are capped by evidence and can be overridden by a fatal blocker.
- Small profitable niches are judged against the founder's goal, not venture TAM.
- Founder labor, support burden, platform concentration, and cash cycle are first-class dimensions.
- Customer Research, Competitor Brief, Chrome, and ready integrations are optional parallel routes.
- External writes, outreach, spending, publishing, and deployment require separate user approval.
