# Fictional Example — Founder-Fit Idea Portfolio

This example is fictional. Every number and customer signal below is part of the
example input; it is not a market claim.

## Example input

- Founder: six years running onboarding operations for boutique marketing agencies
- Capacity: eight hours/week; $600 test budget; wants first revenue within 30 days
- Access: fourteen agency owners in a warm network; 1,900 relevant newsletter subscribers
- Assets: onboarding audit checklist, three original templates, two anonymized case studies with permission
- Evidence supplied: four owners described delayed kickoff and repeated missing-client-input problems; two previously paid the founder for adjacent operational help
- Boundary: no custom software build before paid demand; no handling client credentials

## Executive recommendation

- **Primary candidate:** Fixed-price Client Onboarding Rescue audit
- **Why it leads:** strongest founder proof, warm buyer access, sellable manually,
  and delivery can create reusable benchmark data without a software build
- **Fastest credible test:** offer five warm owners a paid, fixed-scope audit with
  a predeclared price and delivery boundary
- **Asymmetric option:** quarterly anonymized onboarding benchmark for agencies
- **Do not pursue yet:** full onboarding SaaS; no evidence that software rather
  than process design is the missing solution
- **Status:** Evidence-informed opportunity ranking, not validated demand

## Primary idea card

### Client Onboarding Rescue audit

- **First buyer and trigger:** owner of a 5–20 person agency after a delayed or
  chaotic client kickoff
- **Painful job:** collect required inputs, align owners, and start delivery
  without repeated chasing
- **Current alternative:** founder-managed email, forms, spreadsheets, and ad-hoc reminders
- **Smallest paid offer:** 90-minute workflow audit plus a prioritized repair map
  and two adapted templates; no implementation or credential handling
- **Price hypothesis:** $650, based only on the founder's adjacent paid work;
  exact willingness to pay remains untested
- **Founder advantage:** direct operational experience, permissioned proof, and
  fourteen warm prospects
- **Reusable component:** scoring method, issue taxonomy, report structure, and
  anonymized benchmark fields
- **Estimated load:** one client 3.5 hours; ten clients/month 35 hours before
  further standardization; thirty clients/month is outside current solo capacity
- **Primary assumption:** owners will pay for diagnosis before buying implementation
- **Test:** send a personalized offer to ten representative warm owners over seven
  days; cap at five calls and two audit slots
- **Precommitted result:** proceed to delivery learning if at least two buy at the
  stated price; inconclusive if fewer than eight qualified owners see the offer;
  fail the current offer if eight or more see it and none requests a buying conversation
- **Main risk:** owners may see diagnosis as free sales discovery
- **Fallback:** sell a narrower kickoff-delay review tied to one recent project

## Seven-day learning plan

1. Confirm the last delayed-onboarding event with five owners using past-behavior questions.
2. Show the exact fixed-scope audit and price to ten qualified warm prospects.
3. Record exposure, replies, buying conversations, objections, and payments separately.
4. Review against the precommitted rule; do not reinterpret likes or compliments as demand.
