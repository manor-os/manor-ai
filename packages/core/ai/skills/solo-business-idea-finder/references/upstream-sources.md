## Deliberate Manor adaptations

- Separate idea generation from idea review so semantic routing can select the
  user's actual intent.
- Prefer founder access, operating load, fast evidence, and downside limits over
  venture-scale market thresholds.
- Keep Workflow, subagents, startup databases, and connected accounts optional.
- Reuse installed Manor `customer-research` and `competitor-brief` only when they
  materially answer a named evidence gap.
- Make scores obey evidence caps and forbid describing idea generation as demand validation.
