# Starter Idea Library

Use this library only for fast random starters, UI idea cards, or when founder
context is too thin to personalize the first screen. These are original
candidate hypotheses derived from the operating patterns in
`opc-case-patterns.md`; they are not copied case studies and do not carry the
demand or revenue of the source businesses. None of the 18 starters is itself
a verified profitable business. Public operating or revenue evidence belongs
only to the named source cases and supports the reusable pattern, not the new
buyer-and-job combination.

Sample without replacement. Prefer candidates that match known founder skills,
audience, ecosystem access, and time constraints. Replace the starter with a
freshly researched candidate when current evidence or founder context is
available.

This is a small audited UI seed set, not the total idea supply. When the user
asks for fresh or additional ideas, generate new buyer-and-job combinations
from the operating patterns and current evidence instead of cycling only these
18 entries. Explain every card in plain language: buyer, delivered outcome, and
how the business would charge.

## marketplace-photo-studio

- **Model:** Browser-local utility
- **Idea:** Product photo batch tool
- **First buyer:** Independent Etsy and Shopify sellers with growing catalogs
- **Promise:** Crop, clean, resize, and export a full product-image set in one browser workflow
- **How it earns:** Monthly subscription or one-time desktop license
- **Solo leverage:** Client-side processing limits storage and compute while one reusable workflow serves many sellers
- **First test:** Process ten real listings manually, then pre-sell a browser prototype to five sellers

## spreadsheet-margin-copilot

- **Model:** Vertical micro-SaaS
- **Idea:** Import quote profit checker
- **First buyer:** Small importers that price shipments in spreadsheets
- **Promise:** Detect missing fees, stale exchange rates, and margin leaks before a quote is sent
- **How it earns:** Per-audit fee or monthly team subscription
- **Solo leverage:** A narrow rules-and-AI layer can sit on top of the files buyers already use
- **First test:** Audit five anonymized quote sheets and ask two teams to pay for a repeatable check

## workspace-approval-addon

- **Model:** Platform marketplace add-on
- **Idea:** Forms approval and reminder add-on
- **First buyer:** Small property and facilities teams using Forms and Sheets
- **Promise:** Turn a form submission into a tracked approval, reminder, and decision log without a new system
- **How it earns:** Monthly fee per company or approval workflow
- **Solo leverage:** Reuse an existing platform's accounts, documents, and marketplace discovery
- **First test:** Build one Forms-to-approval demo and interview ten Workspace-heavy operators

## open-core-log-scrubber
