# Social and community destinations

Match the community's reason for opening the platform. A promotional claim that works in a brand account may fail in a peer discussion. When the request involves reading, posting, or current community rules, use an available authorized platform/browser route; do not pretend the rules were checked from memory.

| Destination | Audience expectation | Draft structure | CTA or handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Xiaohongshu/RedNote | Searchable recommendations and practical experience | Specific cover/title, occasion or problem, real details and evidence, useful conclusion; distinguish brand copy from personal experience | Save, search, inquire, visit a valid store/product path |
| Pinterest | Visual planning and future action | Useful image/collection with clear subject and landing-page match | Visit the relevant page or product collection |
| LinkedIn | Professional insight and business proof | State the operator/customer problem, one defensible point of view, evidence, and a discussion or demo next step | Qualified conversation, page visit, demo inquiry |
| X | Fast idea exchange and timely reaction | One clear point or concise thread with source and a reason to care now | Relevant reply, source visit, product inquiry |
| Reddit or specialist forum | Community problem solving | Check the specific community rules; answer the question fully and disclose affiliation where relevant | Useful reply first; link or promotion only when allowed |
| WeChat/official account | Existing relationship or deliberate subscription | A clear topic, sustained explanation or offer, consistent brand/source facts | Appropriate article, inquiry, mini-program or reservation path |

For each destination provide a title/hook, native post outline, visual asset list, one full draft when requested, comment/reply handling note, CTA, and metric. Cross-posting means adapting the same truth, not copying one caption verbatim. Do not invent first-person visits, purchases, reviews, customer quotes, or a community endorsement.

Before publication, verify current post formats, disclosure rules, account capabilities, community-specific policy, and link placement in the actual interface. [LinkedIn's marketing guidance](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/how-to-market-on-linkedin) is a starting point for professional audience content; it does not replace a check of the specific account or current placement. An installed `platform-native-social` skill may handle its supported social publishing workflow, but its presence must be checked; this platform overlay remains useful even when no publishing route is connected.
