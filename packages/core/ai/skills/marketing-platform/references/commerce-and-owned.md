# Commerce and owned destinations

Distinguish a marketplace product detail page from an owned store, and an owned message channel from a paid placement inside a commerce platform. The platform determines the presentation and link path; the method determines whether the user is acquiring, converting, retaining, or reactivating customers.

| Destination | Buyer task | Required asset/claim alignment | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon, Tmall/JD and similar marketplaces | Compare exact products and variants | SKU/variant, real imagery, attributes, price and fulfillment as currently shown; review provenance | Add to cart or purchase |
| Shopify or owned product page | Evaluate and transact | Ad-to-page promise, variant, benefits/proof, shipping/returns when stated, checkout path | Purchase |
| TikTok Shop or social commerce | Move from discovery to product listing | Video/creator claim and product listing match; promotion terms are current | Product detail view, purchase |
| Owned website or app | Research or complete a task | Landing copy, proof, form/booking/cart and analytics event agree | Signup, inquiry, booking, sale |
| Email or SMS | Continue a consented relationship | Segment, sender identity, timing, offer and destination match; relevant unsubscribe/suppression | Open is secondary; reply, click, order or retention is primary |

For physical products, list the exact variant, real product photo or approved concept, differentiation, substantiation, price/availability source, CTA URL or store route, and post-click page. For SaaS, align marketing copy with actual UI and current plan/integration facts. For restaurants, align offer copy with the menu and booking/order channel that can fulfill it.

Return a destination-specific content block: title/subject or page hero, benefits ordered by buyer concern, evidence element, visual needs, CTA, fulfillment/consent checks, and measurement event. A product listing, email, and landing page should use shared facts but different copy structures.

Before live edits or sending, inspect current marketplace/category rules, account fields, consent state, deliverability requirements, and valid destination links. Do not infer that the business has permission to message a contact from the mere existence of an address or customer record.
