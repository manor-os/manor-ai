# Search and maps destinations

Search and map surfaces serve **existing intent**. Separate informational, comparison, local visit, and transactional queries. Use the user's actual service area and offer; do not assume a nearby audience or operating hours.

| Destination | Content unit | Critical consistency | Conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google/Baidu organic search | Useful page matching query and stage | Page claim, title, body, availability and CTA agree | Qualified visit, lead, sale |
| Google Search Ads or similar search ads | Query/ad/landing-page chain | Ad promise matches landing page, real offer, eligible location and tracked event | Qualified lead, call, sale |
| Google Business Profile | Accurate profile and eligible update/offer/event | Address, hours, category, phone, menu/service, dates and terms | Call, directions, booking, visit |
| Apple Maps and local directories | Accurate listing | Name, location, contact, categories and action link | Directions, call, booking |
| Meituan/Dianping and local marketplaces | Store/menu/service listing or offer | Actual menu, price/availability, fulfillment area, booking/order terms | Booking, order, visit |

For a local business, inventory canonical name, address/service area, contact, hours, category, reservation/order path, current photos, menu/services, and offer dates before writing a listing. A restaurant may need separate dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and catering paths. A product search campaign may require an exact SKU and a product-specific landing page. Do not draft an offer post as if it were live inventory.

Provide a query/intent cluster, destination page/listing, message/asset, CTA, verification list, primary outcome event, and measurement source. If the method is SEO/local discovery, send these to `marketing-method` for the work plan. If the method is paid search, use the paid-method budget/measurement contract.

Check current account and policy details before execution. [Google Business Profile's official post guide](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en) distinguishes updates, offers, and events. [Google Ads' official campaign-type guide](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2567043?hl=en) shows that format and placement depend on the selected campaign type. These are reference points; verify the exact available options in the connected account.
