# Video destinations

## Choose the viewer job

Short feeds such as TikTok/Douyin, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Kuaishou favor discovery from a feed. A long YouTube or Bilibili video can support search, comparison, education, and a more complete demonstration. Do not assume one cut fits both jobs. The same source footage may be reused, but structure and CTA should be re-edited.

| Destination | Build the creative around | Product example | Restaurant example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok/Douyin or Kuaishou | A visible situation or payoff before explanation, a credible human/process demonstration, and one next action | Show the real task, then an accurate product result | Show the actual food, service moment, location/occasion, then the reservation/order path |
| Instagram Reels | A strong visual identity and an understandable sequence that survives muted viewing | Product in context with legible UI or physical detail | Food, space, people and occasion with an exact venue/booking cue |
| YouTube Shorts | A self-contained answer or mini-demonstration that can lead to deeper content | One use case and evidence, then full demo or signup | One menu/occasion question, then location or booking details |
| YouTube/Bilibili long-form | A truthful title/thumbnail promise, chapters or steps, and enough evidence for consideration | Problem, workflow, live demonstration, limits, CTA | Menu/venue/service walkthrough, visit logistics, booking CTA |

## Production handoff

Specify destination, audience, format, duration goal, source assets, one-message hook, opening proof, middle demonstration, ending CTA, on-screen text, captions, voice/music role, landing or booking destination, and measurement event. If a finished video is requested, this is an input to `video-production`; it owns shot canvas, media generation, edit, and render. Platform guidance does not manufacture footage.

Before publishing or buying ads, verify current account-specific format, aspect ratio, duration, safe area, audio/music rights, branded-content/advertising disclosures, caption behavior, and linking options in the platform interface or official help. [TikTok's official creative guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads?redirected=1) discusses a hook/body/close structure and safe composition; treat it as guidance, not a guaranteed performance formula. For YouTube ad placements, check [official YouTube ad format guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2375464?hl=en) when the method is paid.

Do not optimize for views alone when the objective is visits, qualified leads, orders, or bookings. State the gap between an in-platform view and a measured business action.
