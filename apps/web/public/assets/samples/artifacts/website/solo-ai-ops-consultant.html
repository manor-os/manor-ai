FAQ

Short answers before we talk.

Do we need to replace our current tools?

Usually not. The goal is to improve the way your existing stack works together. I focus on practical integrations before recommending any new platform.

What kinds of workflows are a good fit?

Recurring workflows with clear inputs and outputs are best: lead routing, customer follow-up, internal reporting, recruiting coordination, support triage, content operations, and document generation.

How do you handle quality and approvals?

AI should not silently make important decisions. I build approval checkpoints, logging, fallback paths, and documentation so your team stays in control.
