Quality standards

Operational checks applied to every customer-visible recommendation and internal handoff.

Q

Accurate context

No routing or renewal recommendation without current account stage, last interaction, owner, open issues, and customer objective.

S

Safe commitments

Agents can draft next steps, but cannot approve credits, pricing, security claims, contract terms, or delivery guarantees.

C

Customer clarity

Every handoff includes a plain-language summary, expected response window, owner, and what the customer should do next.
